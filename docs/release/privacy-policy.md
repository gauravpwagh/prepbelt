# PrepBelt privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

PrepBelt is an exam-preparation app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## What PrepBelt stores

PrepBelt stores on your phone what you put into it:
- your name, if you give one;
- your exam and study time;
- the sources you add (PDF files, books, links, videos);
- your survey answers;
- your tasks;
- your reviews and their timings.

PDF files you add are kept in a study folder you choose on your phone (on Android; on iPhone they stay in the app's own storage). PrepBelt also saves a backup of your progress into that folder, at the frequency you set in Settings. The backup contains the information listed above, but not the PDF files.

## What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you choose to send it:

- **No network:** PrepBelt does not send any of your information to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. It works without an internet connection.
- **Cloud backup off:** Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for PrepBelt.
- **Your exports:** when you use **Export a backup** or **Export diagnostics** in Settings, PrepBelt creates a file and saves it where you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you. The diagnostics file contains the app version, counts of stored items, recent kinds of changes and settings. It never contains file contents, titles or your name.
- **Opening a source:** when you open a source, PrepBelt hands the file or link to another app on your phone (for example a PDF viewer or browser). That app's own privacy policy applies.
- **Sharing in:** when you share a PDF or link into PrepBelt from another app, PrepBelt receives only that item.

## Notifications

If you allow it, PrepBelt shows one daily study reminder. It is scheduled on your phone; no server is involved.

## Children

PrepBelt is meant for students preparing for exams and is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your information

Uninstalling PrepBelt deletes everything it stored inside the app. Your study folder, its PDFs and its backups stay on your phone until you delete them. Within the app, you can remove sources and tasks at any time.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
